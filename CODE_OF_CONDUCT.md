# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. We are committed to
providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone
who participates in the Model Diet community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and respect toward other people.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, discriminatory or
derogatory comments, personal attacks, intimidation, publishing someone's
private information without permission, and other conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and other community interactions representing Model
Diet.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove or edit contributions that violate this code and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation, depending on the
severity and context. Maintainers will communicate the reasons for enforcement
when it is appropriate and safe to do so.

To report a concern, contact **@Spinachboul** privately through the contact
channel listed on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Spinachboul). If
you cannot reach a maintainer privately, open an issue asking for a private
reporting route; do not include sensitive details in that issue.
